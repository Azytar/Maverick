MAVERICK / WORKSPACE REFERENCE

A workspace with a point of view.

COLUMN RIBBON
Tiled clients enter independent columns. Adding a column extends the
workspace instead of shrinking the existing composition.

CAMERA
Directional focus and page snapping move the viewport to the focused
column. The workspace continues beyond the visible region.

FLOATING
A floating client belongs to the workspace but not to the tiled ribbon.
Its screen-space geometry remains independent while tiles move beneath it.

REAL CLIENTS
The showcase launches normal X11 applications and inspects Maverick's
public control socket. This document is a local offline fixture.
